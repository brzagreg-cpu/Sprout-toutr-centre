About Chante

Hello! I'm Chante, a passionate English educator based in South Africa with over 12 years of experience helping children fall in love with the English language. I hold a B.Ed in English Education and a TESOL certification, and I specialise in creating fun, structured, and confidence-building lessons for young learners aged 4–14.

My students come from Brazil, China, Portugal and beyond — and I pride myself on making every child feel safe, encouraged, and excited to learn. Every lesson is tailored to your child's unique goals, learning style, and pace.
